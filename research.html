Research

Researcher, University of North Texas, Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Lab of Dr. Wonbong Choi
September 2025 - Present

- Supervised by Ph.D. candidate Chaekyung Kim

- Granted a $4,000 scholarship for Summer 2026 research

- Research durable, fire-resistant zinc-ion batteries with polymer electrolytes; fabricate and test pouch cells to optimize materials

- Lead an independent project on stabilizing the zinc anode–electrolyte interface through surface engineering: texture zinc anodes by mechanical abrasion and HCl etching to study how surface roughness affects adhesion to polyacrylamide (PAM) hydrogel electrolyte and battery performance

- Found that moderately rough surfaces give the strongest hydrogel adhesion and best cycling, and that the type of roughness matters more than its magnitude; currently investigating the zinc passivation layer as the mechanism and drafting a first-author manuscript

- Fort Worth Regional Science and Engineering Fair (February 2026): Second Place, Energy and Transport Category, and Second Place, Lockheed Martin Special Award, Senior Division, for “Flexible and Non-Flammable Zinc-Ion Battery Through Polymer Electrolytes” (view poster PDF)

- Collected and analyzed data using X-ray diffraction (XRD), Raman spectroscopy, electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS), cyclic voltammetry (CV), thermogravimetric analysis (TGA), battery cycling, scanning electron microscopy (SEM), atomic force microscopy (AFM), and contact angle measurement
